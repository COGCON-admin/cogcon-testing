ⓘ COGCON® Reference

What We're Measuring

♥

Heart Rate (Beat to Beat)

What it is

Beat-to-beat heart rate derived from the time between consecutive heartbeats (RR intervals), not a rolling average.

Why it matters

Beat-to-beat data reveals the variability pattern — the natural fluctuation in time between beats that indicates how well the autonomic nervous system is regulating under stress.

What to look for

A jagged, variable trace indicates good regulation. A flat, rigid trace at high HR indicates stress dominance.

〜

HR Coherence

What it is

A measure of heart rate variability derived from the rolling 60-beat RMSSD of RR intervals, normalised to a 0–100% scale.

Why it matters

Coherence reflects autonomic nervous system regulation. High coherence means the candidate is physiologically regulated. Low coherence means stress is dominating and cognitive performance is at risk.

What to look for

Green trace = regulated. Red trace = dysregulated. Watch for colour shifts at scenario entry and during high-demand moments.

↑

EPOC (Estimated)

What it is

Excess Post-exercise Oxygen Consumption — a measure of accumulated physiological cost. Displayed as an estimate derived from heart rate data.

Why it matters

EPOC reflects the total physiological load on the candidate. Rising EPOC under moderate physical activity indicates stress activation is compounding the physical demand.

What to look for

Rapid EPOC rise at scenario entry = high stress activation. Sustained elevation = accumulated load affecting cognitive capacity.

Note

Precise EPOC is available post-session from Firstbeat XLSX export.

⚡

SEB Windows

What it is

Periods where coherence drops below 40% for 5 or more seconds — automatically detected and flagged.

Why it matters

SEB windows are the moments where the candidate most needed to regulate. They are retrospective coaching anchors — where SEB would have had highest impact.

What to look for

Green bar on screen left edge = SEB window active. Count and timing of windows drives the retrospective debrief.

HR Trace Colour Thresholds

GREEN / YELLOW — Regulated

Coherence > 70%
Autonomic regulation stable. Cognitive capacity protected. Optimal performance state.

ORANGE — Moderate

Coherence 40–70%
Regulation present but under load. Monitor for further drop. SEB proactively here prevents dysregulation.

AMBER — Low / SEB Window

Coherence 20–40%
Stress dominating. Cognitive performance at risk. SEB window active — highest leverage intervention point.

RED — Dysregulated

Coherence < 20%
Significant dysregulation. Working memory and decision quality impaired. Immediate SEB required.

Note: Colour is coherence, height is HR. A red trace at moderate HR is more concerning than an orange trace at high HR.

SEB Cycle — Operational Reset Tool

S

Self Check

Rapid internal awareness check: heart rate, emotion, focus, information overload. Creates space between stimulus and response.

E

Eyes Up

Deliberate widening of the attentional field. Breaks fixation. Restores scanning and situational awareness.

B

Breathing & Body Language

Tactical breath to downshift arousal. Body language as performance control — squared, upright, deliberate. Not cosmetic.

OPS

Operational Performance Check

Am I in an optimal Operational Performance State? If yes — continue. If no — run SEB again before acting.

WIN

What's Important Now

Priority anchor. Compresses attention onto the next correct action. The bridge between regulation and decision execution.

Pre-Scenario Default Protocol

1. Execute SEB the moment job comms come through — by default, not when stress is noticed.

2. Apply multiple cycles in the truck if stress or worry builds during the wait.

3. Final SEB in the last 90 seconds before exiting the truck.

4. Identify WIN before the door opens.
